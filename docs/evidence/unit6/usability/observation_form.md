## Task log

For each task record: completion status (**independent** = no help; **assisted** = completed after a hint, write the hint; **failed** = not completed or incorrect answer), observed difficulty (what the participant did, hesitated over, clicked wrongly, said), the participant's own words, and optionally the duration in whole seconds.
